Uttar Pradesh MSME Subsidy Framework

Understanding benefits, regional divisions and first-come-first-serve portal queues.

Regional Subsidies splits:

  • Bundelkhand & Purvanchal (Eastern UP): Eligible for the highest capital grants (15% - 25%) and 100% stamp duty exemption on industrial land.
  • Madhyanchal (Central UP) & Paschimanchal (Western UP): Eligible for up to 10% - 20% capital subsidies and 75% stamp duty exemptions.
  • Gautam Buddh Nagar (Noida) & Ghaziabad: Eligible for up to 10% - 15% capital subsidies and 50% stamp duty exemptions.
  • Women-led Projects: Eligible for 100% stamp duty exemption statewide, regardless of regional borders.
