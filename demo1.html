COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you reach out

Do you charge any upfront fee?

No. Our advisory fee is disclosed and agreed before engagement begins. We don't charge processing fees that banks already charge.

Are you a bank or NBFC?

Neither. We're an independent loan consultancy and corporate finance advisory. We work across multiple banks and NBFCs to find the best terms for your profile.

Which areas do you serve?

Primarily Ghaziabad, Delhi NCR, and Uttar Pradesh -- though many of our equity and incentive services apply across India.

How long does the process take?

Depends on the product. Unsecured loans can be sanctioned in 2-3 days; project loans and IPO advisory take 2-4 weeks to several months.

Can you help if my account is already NPA?

Yes. We offer One-Time Settlement (OTS) negotiation and bridge refinancing for viable businesses with stressed accounts.

Do you handle government subsidy applications?

Yes -- for UP state schemes including TUS (machinery subsidy), MSME Promotion Policy (capital subsidy + stamp duty), and MSMY (youth self-employment).
